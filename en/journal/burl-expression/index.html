Birch burl: our first choice.

A tree in the forest, a material in the hand. Looking at the same birch from different distances brings small discoveries that lead towards our first pen.

EZOWOOD JOURNAL / 02

In this story

01 — A name known in the forest, now on the desk.02 — Where the eye rests within burl.03 — Getting to know one model, slowly.

A name known in the forest, now on the desk.

Perhaps a landscape of pale bark first introduced you to birch. Knowing the tree and using its wood in everyday life are experiences at different distances. A trunk seen from afar becomes a tool held in the hand. It is a chance to know a tree with a familiar name a little more closely.

Shirakaba, also called shirakanba in Japanese, grows in Hokkaido, central and northern Honshu, and north-eastern Asia. Learning about the tree itself reveals other landscapes beyond the material of a pen. We hope to find in a writing instrument something like the pleasure of bringing home the name of a tree, as well as the name of a place.

Where the eye rests within burl.

Following a straight grain and exploring the character of burl are slightly different ways of looking. Among interwoven lines, an eye following one may be invited towards another. Some areas open into broad colour; others gather into fine patterns. Part of the interest is that there is more to see after a first look.

We chose a birch burl pen for our first product concept. Some may see a picture within a pattern; others may enjoy it simply as colour and line. Looking at the same surface, each person may notice a different place. Together with partner workshops and craftspeople, we hope to introduce a material that invites this personal way of seeing as a tool for everyday use.

Getting to know one model, slowly.

A wide range of options may not be the only pleasure in choosing. Look at one pen as a whole, explore its grain, and imagine where you would use it. Changing the place you look gradually adds to what you know. We are beginning our first collection with one click-action ballpoint model.

How would it look beside your diary? If you gave it to someone, what would you want to say? Begin with the material, then let your thoughts move into everyday life. A conversation about wood can become an interest in a person’s days, or in the workshops that bring the material into use. As a brand responsible for developing and presenting products, we value that kind of connection.

Burl and landscape images are references. Actual material, product, and partner-workshop photography is being prepared.
