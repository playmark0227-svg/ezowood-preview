GIFT / 04

Give the reason you chose it.

The story accompanying a gift need not be elaborate. A few words can hold the time spent choosing with someone in mind.

EZOWOOD JOURNAL / 04

In this story

01 — Find the connection with the person.02 — Prepare a few words.03 — Leave something to enjoy after the giving.

Find the connection with the person.

Someone who treasures wooden belongings. Someone who remembers time spent in Hokkaido. Someone who enjoys putting words on paper. A reason for choosing a gift can be found in your memories of the person, as well as in a product description. The choice of a pen begins by recalling a conversation or an object once seen on their desk.

Rather than trying to predict every preference perfectly, imagine one scene that feels like them: writing a letter, taking a note at work, putting travel plans into a notebook. When you can picture how you hope it will be used, a personal reason joins the way you look at colours and patterns.

Prepare a few words.

“It brought back that journey.” “You said you liked the feel of wood.” A reason for choosing may need no more words than these. Alongside any explanation of the product, tell the person why you wanted to give it to them. Those words connect the object to its recipient.

Choose a sheet of writing paper and leave just a brief thought. A small touch in your own words needs no fixed format. If you rarely write letters, the handwriting itself may become part of the gift. Even the time before handing over a pen offers somewhere to place a feeling.

Leave something to enjoy after the giving.

The recipient looks at the grain and says which part they like. Their eye may settle somewhere different from the place you first noticed. A natural wooden tool offers the pleasure of looking together in the moment. Rather than telling every part of its background, it can be good to hear what the other person sees.

Once a gift is given, where it rests in the day and when it is used belong to its new owner. Perhaps it can also carry an introduction to Hokkaido’s materials and the work that shapes them into tools. We hope you will enjoy choosing it, imagining a quiet place taking shape within someone else’s life.
