ORDER / 05

The time before a pen comes to you.

Regular orders are currently closed. Rather than hurrying towards the next release, begin with time to discover the material, the makers and the ways you might use a pen. Here are a few things to consider before welcoming one.

EZOWOOD JOURNAL / 05

In this story

01 — Consider where it will belong.02 — Get to know the material and the makers.03 — Check the next announcement.

Consider where it will belong.

As you choose a pen, think of the notebooks and diaries you already own. Will it sit beside the things you use at work every day, or be kept for writing letters at home? Considering a place for a new tool is also a chance to look again at how you write now.

Your preferences for appearance and your hopes for use: a brief note of both can help when you check the product information later. You need not decide on colour and grain alone. Learn the details you need a little at a time, and consider whether it suits you. That time, too, is part of the experience before a pen arrives.

Get to know the material and the makers.

What character can be found in Hokkaido’s burl wood? What do the workshops that bring it into use look for? Even while orders are closed, there is time to discover the material and the work. Look at photographs, learn a tree’s name, imagine a scene of writing. The time before choosing can offer its own way into knowing a pen.

Ezowood Hokkaido is a brand that develops products with partner workshops and craftspeople, and shares their background. Begin with a grain that catches your interest, and consider what you might like to ask the maker. As we introduce more work, we hope to create a place where you can also encounter the perspectives of the people behind it.

Check the next announcement.

The reopening date and next dispatch month have not yet been decided. We will announce the price and sales terms once they are ready to share. If you are considering a gift for a particular date, it matters to know not only whether orders are open, but also when dispatch is planned and what delivery conditions apply. Check the schedule against the time you need it before deciding.

Preparing to welcome a pen is not only about proceeding to purchase. Note what drew you to the material, observe a familiar tool, make a little time with paper. We hope you will enjoy what is already possible within your everyday life while waiting for the next announcement.
