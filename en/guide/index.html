QUESTIONS

Before welcoming a pen.

Explore the material, made-to-order plans, and the details that matter to you.

20 questions

Q.What kind of pen will be offered first?
A.

We plan one model: a click-action ballpoint with a birch burl barrel. The initial release will not offer a choice of wood species or fittings. Shape, finish, dimensions, weight, refills and other details are still being confirmed.

Q.Will I receive a pen with the same grain as the photograph?
A.

The photographs are illustrative references. You will not receive the physical pen pictured; grain, colour and burl patterns differ from one pen to another. There is currently no option to specify a grain pattern or choose from individual pens.

Q.Where in Hokkaido does the wood come from?
A.

We are a brand that aims to share the value of Hokkaido’s burl wood through product development with partner workshops and craftspeople. Details of the birch burl’s precise origin, the timber’s history and proof-of-origin documents will be provided as each is verified.

Q.Do the making images show the actual workshop?
A.

The current images are illustrative. We plan to photograph the actual product and replace them. Our approach is to work with partner workshops and craftspeople on production; the images are not documentary introductions to a particular workshop or maker responsible for the pen.

Q.What is the price?
A.

The selling price has not yet been decided. We plan a tax-inclusive price covering the pen, box and delivery, but the final terms, including the destinations covered by delivery, are unconfirmed. We will announce them before launch.

Q.When will orders reopen, and when will the next dispatch be?
A.

Regular orders are currently closed, and the reopening date and next dispatch month are undecided. We will share further information when it is ready. Quantities considered during the launch planning do not represent an allocation currently open for reservation.

Q.How do you plan to sell the pen when orders reopen?
A.

Our first product concept is one click-action birch burl ballpoint model. For made-to-order sales, the plan is to proceed to production with the workshop after payment, but orders are currently closed. Please check the formal announcement at reopening for the price, payment methods, production and dispatch timing, and terms for changes.

Q.Can I buy from this site now?
A.

Regular orders are currently closed. This site is also a preview for reviewing the design and information; no actual orders, payments or production bookings are made. The notification form neither registers your details nor sends anything.

Q.Can I change or cancel an order?
A.

Terms for changes before or after production begins, cancellations, returns and exchanges are in preparation. Please do not infer what is possible simply from the made-to-order format. Check the terms to be published before launch.

Q.How long will it take to arrive?
A.

The next dispatch month is undecided. We will announce the planned production and dispatch schedule when orders reopen. Arrival also depends on the destination and delivery conditions, so please check the dispatch plan alongside the delivery terms.

Q.Is there a delivery charge?
A.

We plan to include delivery in the price. However, delivery regions, additional charges for remote islands and other destinations, and overseas arrangements are unconfirmed. This does not mean delivery to every address will be free of additional charges.

Q.Do you deliver overseas?
A.

Overseas destinations, delivery charges, currencies and customs arrangements are undecided. We are considering introducing the brand overseas, including Taiwan, but do not currently promise delivery to any of those regions.

Q.Can I choose a delivery date or place an urgent order?
A.

The next dispatch month, date-specific delivery and expedited production or dispatch are undecided. As regular orders are currently closed, we are not accepting orders based on arrival on a particular day. Please wait for the formal announcement.

Q.How should I care for the pen each day?
A.

As general guidance, wipe gently with a soft, dry cloth and avoid water, high temperatures and prolonged direct sunlight. Detailed methods suited to the finish are in preparation. Please do not apply oils or chemical products at your own discretion.

Q.Which refill does it use?
A.

Compatible model numbers and replacement instructions are still being checked. A click-action format does not mean that a commercially available refill will necessarily fit. We will provide details once the specifications are confirmed, including sources and any related items offered for sale.

Q.Will repairs or a guarantee be available?
A.

Repair availability, scope, charges, guarantees and other conditions are in preparation. We do not currently promise free assistance or a lifetime guarantee. We also intend to explain how to contact us about a concern before launch.

Q.Are all changes in the wood treated as natural character?
A.

Natural differences in grain and colour are considered separately from cracks or faults in the writing mechanism. We will not dismiss every condition as a characteristic of natural wood. Fault assessment and the terms of any response will be stated before launch.

Q.Can I choose gift wrapping or a card?
A.

We plan to supply the pen in a box, but the box material and shape, wrapping, card details and charges are unconfirmed. The images are not samples of the actual packaging. We will explain the available options before launch.

Q.Can the pen be personalised?
A.

Engraving is a possibility for the future. The initial release will not include an option to engrave a name or message. Whether it will be offered, the lettering, position, charges and lead times remain undecided.

Q.Can you send it directly to the recipient?
A.

Direct delivery to a gift recipient and omitting documents that reveal the price are unconfirmed. Please check the information to be provided before the actual launch, including how to enter the delivery address and what will be enclosed.
