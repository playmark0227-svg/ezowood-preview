WHY WE EXIST

Bring less familiar value into everyday life.

There is remarkable wood, and there are people devoted to their craft. Connecting them with users also calls for someone to share their stories. We are a planning and storytelling brand connecting Hokkaido materials, partner workshops, and users; we do not carve the pens ourselves.

QUESTIONS

Before welcoming a pen.

Explore the material, made-to-order plans, and the details that matter to you.

Q.What kind of pen will be offered first?
A.

We plan one model: a click-action ballpoint with a birch burl barrel. The initial release will not offer a choice of wood species or fittings. Shape, finish, dimensions, weight, refills and other details are still being confirmed.

Q.Will I receive a pen with the same grain as the photograph?
A.

The photographs are illustrative references. You will not receive the physical pen pictured; grain, colour and burl patterns differ from one pen to another. There is currently no option to specify a grain pattern or choose from individual pens.

Q.How do you plan to sell the pen when orders reopen?
A.

Our first product concept is one click-action birch burl ballpoint model. For made-to-order sales, the plan is to proceed to production with the workshop after payment, but orders are currently closed. Please check the formal announcement at reopening for the price, payment methods, production and dispatch timing, and terms for changes.

Q.How long will it take to arrive?
A.

The next dispatch month is undecided. We will announce the planned production and dispatch schedule when orders reopen. Arrival also depends on the destination and delivery conditions, so please check the dispatch plan alongside the delivery terms.

Q.Can I choose gift wrapping or a card?
A.

We plan to supply the pen in a box, but the box material and shape, wrapping, card details and charges are unconfirmed. The images are not samples of the actual packaging. We will explain the available options before launch.
